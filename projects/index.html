Space and Aerospace

Heterogeneous Data Fusion for Space Domain Awareness (SDA)

May 2026

Excellence Award, 2026 Yonsei Aerospace Week Symposium

I built a toolkit that flags unusual satellite orbits from orbital data, checks whether flagged satellites are observable by optical telescopes, and supports adding a third data source: Earth observation imagery. After the symposium, I reviewed my code and found bugs that made the initial results unreliable. Rather than keeping those results, I rewrote the toolkit as a tested package with 15 passing tests and validated it on a small demo catalog of 58 objects.

Spring 2026

Individual project, open-source software course

I built a FastAPI service to store satellite orbit data and score anomalies, along with a Streamlit web app for visualizing orbits and flagging unusual ones. I containerized the API with Docker and deployed both components on AWS EC2.

Drone Swarm Consensus Stability

Fall 2025

Individual project

I used Monte Carlo simulations to study how ten drones reach consensus in the presence of noise. My initial theoretical formula differed from the simulation by about a factor of four. After investigating the discrepancy, I found that the formula was only valid for small step sizes. I corrected the formula, and the revised result matched the simulation within about 1%.

Mars Terrain Hazard Detection

Aug 2025

Excellence Award, robot vision course

I trained YOLOv8 models to detect bedrock, large rocks, sand and soil in Mars images. On a validation set from 322 images, a smaller model performed similarly to a larger one while running faster. However, it struggled to detect large rocks because only eight training images contained them.

Job Posting Competency Matching

Spring 2026

Team project of five, team leader and presenter

We built a system that matches job postings to skills, using 13,394 IT job postings. I checked the data quality, extracted skills, tried a transformer model, and built the demo. A simple TF-IDF + SVM model worked better than the two transformer models we tried, so we used it.
